# Product Design Framework: The Double Diamond

> **Usage**: Load this framework when conducting "Product Planning" or "Feature Design" tasks.

## 1. Phase 1: Discover (Divergent) / 发现阶段
> **Goal**: Understand the problem space. Do not jump to solutions yet.

- **Key Questions**:
  - Who are the users? (Persona Analysis)
  - What are their pain points?
  - What is the context of use?
  - What are competitors doing?

- **Actions**:
  - Conduct User Research
  - Market Analysis
  - Stakeholder Interviews

## 2. Phase 2: Define (Convergent) / 定义阶段
> **Goal**: Define the core problem and scope.

- **Key Questions**:
  - What is the specific problem we are solving?
  - What is NOT in scope? (MVP definition)
  - What does success look like?

- **Actions**:
  - Problem Statement
  - User Journey Mapping
  - Feature Prioritization (MoSCoW method)

## 3. Phase 3: Develop (Divergent) / 开发阶段
> **Goal**: Explore potential solutions.

- **Key Questions**:
  - What are the possible ways to solve this?
  - How might we implement this technically?
  - What are the trade-offs?

- **Actions**:
  - Brainstorming
  - Prototyping
  - Logic Design

## 4. Phase 4: Deliver (Convergent) / 交付阶段
> **Goal**: Finalize the solution.

- **Key Questions**:
  - Does this meet the acceptance criteria?
  - Is it feasible to build?
  - Is the user experience smooth?

- **Actions**:
  - PRD Writing (Use `templates/prd_template.md`)
  - User Story Writing
  - Spec Finalization
